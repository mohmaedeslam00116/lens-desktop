# Evidence-Grounded SWOT Analysis Framework

Methodological framework for conducting empirical SWOT analyses grounded in primary literature, corporate disclosures, and technical documentation.

## Core Rules for Evidence-Grounded SWOT
1. **Zero Unsubstantiated Claims**: Every point in each quadrant must be accompanied by at least one primary source citation `[N]`.
2. **Distinct Categorization**:
   - **Strengths (Internal)**: Documented organizational advantages, IP patents, proprietary datasets, and verified performance leads.
   - **Weaknesses (Internal)**: Technical debt, high unit costs, geographic limitations, missing platform integrations, or customer churn signals.
   - **Opportunities (External)**: Expanding market sectors, emerging compliance mandates, competitor missteps, or platform shifts.
   - **Threats (External)**: Aggressive open-source commoditization, regulatory crackdowns, supply chain friction, or macroeconomic headwinds.
