# Competitive Market Intelligence & Strategic Benchmarking

Use this skill when conducting commercial competitor analysis, market landscape evaluations, product capability benchmarking, and business model assessments.

## Objectives & Core Directives

1. **Objective Commercial Grounding**: Extract empirical pricing models, feature tiers, customer segments, and enterprise security standards from verified market sources.
2. **Tabular Feature Matrix**: Structure capabilities using [references/feature-matrix-template.md](references/feature-matrix-template.md), identifying parity, gaps, and unique differentiators.
3. **Evidence-Grounded SWOT**: Construct rigorous SWOT analyses using [references/swot-framework.md](references/swot-framework.md) with deterministic bracketed citations `[N]` for every strength, weakness, opportunity, and threat.
4. **Moat & Defensibility Assessment**: Evaluate network effects, data flywheels, switching costs, ecosystem lock-in, and intellectual property.

## Step-by-Step Analytical Protocol

### Step 1: Competitor Landscape Mapping
Identify and classify industry participants:
- **Incumbent Leaders**: Dominant market share holders with established distribution channels.
- **Fast-Growing Challengers**: Modern alternatives differentiating on velocity, price, or developer experience.
- **Niche / Point Solutions**: Specialized vendors serving narrow vertical requirements.

### Step 2: Product Capability & Pricing Matrix
Map offerings into `references/feature-matrix-template.md`:
- Document published pricing tiers (Free, Pro, Enterprise, consumption-based).
- Verify deployment models (SaaS, On-Premises, Hybrid, Air-Gapped).
- Audit compliance credentials (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR).

### Step 3: Strategic SWOT Synthesis
Audit each competitor against `references/swot-framework.md`:
- Anchor strengths in verifiable customer proof points and documented performance metrics.
- Expose vulnerabilities in missing integrations, pricing opacity, or scaling bottlenecks.
- Ground market opportunities in emerging regulatory changes or technology inflection points.

### Step 4: Executive Comparison & Takeaways
Summarize findings in an actionable executive brief:
- Present a High-Level Decision Matrix highlighting best-fit scenarios for each evaluated competitor.
- Conclude with clear strategic recommendations and defensibility forecasts.
